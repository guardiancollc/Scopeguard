## Full description (draft)
ScopeGuard helps contractors and subcontractors organize essential job records in one place.

Create and manage invoices, maintain client and project information, document field activity, organize project photos, and prepare change orders for extra work. Eligible plans also include AI-assisted invoice drafting and team tools.

Designed for field and office use, ScopeGuard makes it easier to keep records connected to the jobs they belong to.

Features vary by subscription. An internet connection and a ScopeGuard account are required for cloud functionality.
